What is Metaversal Solutions?
Metaversal Solutions is an AI-first software and digital innovation studio based in Karachi, Pakistan. It designs and engineers web and mobile apps, cloud data platforms, DevOps pipelines, UI/UX, brand design, 3D (Blender) work and AI/ML systems for businesses.
Where is Metaversal Solutions based?
Metaversal Solutions is based in Karachi, Pakistan, and works with clients worldwide.
What services does Metaversal Solutions offer?
Nine services: Cloud Data Engineering, DevOps Engineering, Web Development, App Development, UI/UX Designing, Graphic Designing, Blender Engineering, AI Data Science, Machine Learning.
How does Metaversal Solutions work with clients?
Through a five-phase process (Discover, Design, Build, Launch, Orbit) and three engagement models: fixed-scope project, dedicated crew, orbit support.
