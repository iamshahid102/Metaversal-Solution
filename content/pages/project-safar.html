Overview

Safar is a responsive travel website for Hajj, Umrah and UAE journeys.

A cinematic Makkah video hero leads into travel packages, services, a guided journey timeline, a destination gallery, pilgrim stories and an FAQ. A validated booking enquiry prepares an email for the travel team.

What it does

01

Cinematic hero

A locally stored Makkah video sets the tone from the first second.

02

Packages & services

Travel packages, services and a guided journey timeline.

03

Gallery & stories

Destination gallery and pilgrim stories.

04

Accessible FAQ

An accessible FAQ accordion and reduced-motion support.

05

Booking enquiry

A validated enquiry dialog that prepares an email draft.

06

Static & fast

Dependency-free static build deployed on Vercel.

How it works

  1. Discover

    Visitors watch the hero film and explore packages and destinations.

  2. Learn

    The journey timeline, stories and FAQ answer their questions.

  3. Enquire

    The booking dialog validates details and prepares an email.

  4. Ship

    A small build script verifies assets and publishes the static site.
